════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
MULTI-PAGE STATEMENTS — HOW TO FIND CONTINUATION PAGES
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

For statements spanning multiple pages, include ALL pages:

TYPE 1 — COLUMN OVERFLOW (most common in wide governmental fund tables):
  The first page has the anchor title and the LEFT-SIDE fund columns.
  The next page has NO title, starts directly with RIGHT-SIDE fund column
  headers ("Other Total / Debt Service Governmental Governmental / Funds")
  followed by dollar amounts. This is the continuation — INCLUDE IT.

TYPE 2 — ROW OVERFLOW (common for SNP, SOA, PROP_*):
  Subsequent pages carry "(Continued)" label at top or bottom,
  or repeat the statement title with "(Continued)".
  OR: the page simply continues mid-table (e.g., starts with "Component
  Units" column headers for SNP, or "Net (Expense) Revenues and Changes
  in Net Position" for SOA second-page columns).

STOP INCLUDING pages when:
  • A new distinct statement title appears (not "(Continued)")
  • A "Reconciliation of..." title appears → REJECT-E
  • "(THIS PAGE LEFT BLANK INTENTIONALLY)" → REJECT-C
  • "Notes to financial statements" begins → REJECT-F
