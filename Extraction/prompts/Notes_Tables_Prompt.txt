TARGET 1 — OVERVIEW: entity profile pages
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Return the pages that describe WHAT AND WHERE THE ENTITY IS. Include a page if it carries
any of:
  • the Letter of Transmittal or the "Profile of the Government" / "The Reporting Entity"
    narrative (legal form, year and authority of creation, services provided, enterprise
    activities, principal revenue base);
  • the entity's head-office / administrative address (street, city, state, ZIP) — often on
    the report cover, the Letter of Transmittal letterhead, or the auditor's report
    addressee block;
  • Note 1 "Summary of Significant Accounting Policies" where it defines the REPORTING
    ENTITY and its component units;
  • the Statistical Section "Demographic and Economic Statistics" schedule (population,
    land area) and any "Principal Employers" schedule.

Typically 2 to 6 pages. Do NOT return the whole introductory section — only pages carrying
one of the four kinds of content above.

DO NOT include: the table of contents, the GFOA certificate of achievement, the
organisational chart, or a list of elected officials.

TARGET 2 — CAPITAL_ASSETS: Changes in Capital Assets roll-forward
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
A page qualifies if BOTH conditions are true:

CONDITION 1 — it prints a roll-forward with columns resembling:
    Beginning Balance | Additions | Deletions | Ending Balance
  Accept these printed variants:
    Beginning : "Balance at Oct. 1", "Balance at October 1, 2024", "Beginning Balance",
                "Restated Balance", "Balance <date>"
    Additions : "Additions", "Increases", "Acquisitions"
    Deletions : "Deletions", "Reductions", "Retirements", "Disposals", "Decreases"
    Ending    : "Balance at <FYE>", "Ending Balance"
  A "Transfers" / "Reclassifications" / "Adjustments" column may also be present.

CONDITION 2 — its ROWS are capital-asset categories, i.e. it contains at least one of:
    "Capital assets, not being depreciated" / "not being depreciated"
    "Capital assets, being depreciated" / "being depreciated and amortized"
    "Less accumulated depreciation" / "Less: accumulated depreciation" /
      "Accumulated depreciation and amortization"
    "Construction in progress"
    "Land" together with "Buildings" or "Infrastructure" or "Machinery and equipment"
    "Right-to-use" assets or "SBITA" listed as asset classes
    "capital assets, net"

The schedule is usually inside a note titled "Capital Assets" / "Changes in Capital
Assets" / "Capital Asset Activity", and normally spans 2 to 4 pages covering Governmental
Activities, then Business-type Activities, then Component Units. Return ALL of them.

DO NOT include (these are different tables):
  • the Changes in Long-Term Liabilities roll-forward — its rows are bonds, notes, leases,
    compensated absences, and it has a "Due Within One Year" column. That is DEBT, not
    CAPITAL_ASSETS.
  • a depreciation-expense-by-function schedule (rows are General Government, Public
    Safety, ... with a single expense column and no Beginning/Ending balance).
  • the capital assets line on the Statement of Net Position.
  • a schedule of capital-project commitments or the capital improvement plan.

DISCRIMINATOR: if a roll-forward page has a "Due Within One Year" column → it is DEBT.
If it has asset-class rows and NO "Due Within One Year" column → it is CAPITAL_ASSETS.

SECTION A — NOTES PAGES THAT QUALIFY FOR TAX_BASE
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
During your scan of the Notes to the Financial Statements,
flag a page for TAX_BASE if its text contains ANY of the
following. Check every Notes page — do not stop after finding
the first one.

  GROUP A — tax calendar (dates or periods):
    Any of: "levy date" | "lien date" | "tax lien date" |
    "lien attaches" | "become due and payable" |
    "delinquent on" | "collection period" | "discount period" |
    "discounts are allowed" | "ad valorem taxes are levied" |
    "taxes are due" | "fiscal year for which levied" |
    "tax roll" | "October 1" | "November 1" | "April 1"
    ► A Notes page containing ANY of these phrases = INCLUDE,
      even if it has no dollar amounts.

  GROUP B — assessed or taxable value (needs a figure):
    Any of: "assessed value" | "taxable value" |
    "taxable assessed" | "estimated actual value" |
    "just value" | "appraised value" | "millable value" |
    "market value of the tax base"
    ► A Notes page containing any of these WITH a dollar
      figure or percentage = INCLUDE.

  GROUP C — levy amount (needs a dollar figure):
    Any of: "total tax levy" | "taxes levied" |
    "amount levied" | "levy amount" | "ad valorem levy"
    ► A Notes page containing any of these WITH a dollar
      figure = INCLUDE.

  GROUP D — millage or tax rate (any numeric figure):
    Any of: "millage rate" | "mill levy" | "tax rate" |
    "operating millage" | "debt service millage" |
    "total direct rate" | "mills" | "per $1,000" |
    "per $100" | "maximum ad valorem tax millage" |
    "10 mills" | "limited to" followed by a mill figure
    ► A Notes page containing any of these WITH any
      numeric figure (e.g. "10 mills", "5.276 mills",
      "7.107") = INCLUDE.

NOTES PAGES QUALIFY EVEN WHEN:
  • Tax content is only narrative — no tables, no schedules.
  • The page covers only the current year, not ten years.
  • The same information appears in the Statistical Section.
  • The tax note starts partway down the page after a
    different note (e.g. a budget note ends, then "NOTE 3 —
    Property Taxes" begins on the same physical page).
  • The page has no dollar amounts — dates and rates alone
    are sufficient for Groups A and D.

NOTES PAGES ARE EXCLUDED ONLY WHEN: the only tax reference
is a bare mention of property taxes with no figure, rate,
date, or calendar detail at all — for example, a single
sentence "The County levies ad valorem taxes" with nothing
else tax-specific on the entire page.

SECTION B0 — PHYSICALLY ROTATED PAGES (90° or 270° landscape)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Some PDFs embed one or more pages in landscape orientation
(rotated 90° or 270°). When extracted as plain text, a rotated
page produces one of these garbled patterns:

  PATTERN 1 — single-character-per-line stream:
    Characters appear one per line down the left margin, e.g.:
      F
      i
      s
      c
      a
      l
    Collapse all single characters top-to-bottom into a word
    or phrase before evaluating the page content.

  PATTERN 2 — reversed or mirrored word stream:
    Words or numbers appear in reversed reading order or as a
    single long horizontal string with no spacing. Re-assemble
    the intended text before evaluating.

  PATTERN 3 — numeric columns printed as a single row:
    All values that should be in separate year columns appear
    run together on one line, e.g.:
      63599222 68171229 73503171 79376212 85468864 91725857

MANDATORY RULE FOR ROTATED PAGES:
  (a) NEVER discard a rotated page without first reconstructing
      its text content by collapsing the garbled stream.
  (b) After reconstruction, apply ALL normal schedule-title and
      content matching rules (Sections B, B1, B2, B3, B4) to
      the reconstructed text.
  (c) A rotated page that, after reconstruction, contains any
      of the three target schedule titles OR matches TYPE R1 or
      TYPE R2 content patterns MUST be included in TAX_BASE.
  (d) The absence of a readable title on a rotated page is NOT
      a reason to exclude it — reconstruct first, then decide.
  (e) Include the rotated page's physical page number in the
      TAX_BASE array alongside all other qualifying pages.

SECTION B1 — HORIZONTAL COLUMN-OVERFLOW PAGES
(title-less right-half pages — THE MOST COMMONLY MISSED TYPE)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Reports produced by Workiva and similar tools often split one
wide schedule across TWO physical pages horizontally:

  LEFT PAGE: has the schedule title, entity name, and the
    first group of columns (e.g. Estimated Actual Value
    columns, or fiscal years 2016–2019).
  RIGHT PAGE: has NO schedule title, NO entity name, NO
    "(Continued)" label. It begins directly with column
    sub-headers (e.g. "Taxable Assessed Value" with
    sub-columns "Real Property | Personal Property |
    Centrally Assessed | Total | Total Direct Tax Rate"),
    or with year numbers ("2020  2021  2022  2023  2024
    2025"), followed immediately by numeric data rows.

These right-half pages contain essential data from Schedule A
or Schedule B and MUST be included in TAX_BASE.

QUALIFY A RIGHT-HALF PAGE DIRECTLY by its own content:

  RIGHT-HALF TYPE R1 — Schedule A right half:
    A page qualifies as a Schedule A continuation if it:
    (a) has no printed schedule title or entity name, AND
    (b) begins with any of these column sub-headers:
        "Taxable Assessed Value" | "Taxable Real Property" |
        "Total Taxable" | "Total Direct Tax Rate" |
        "Real Property  Personal Property  Centrally
         Assessed  Total", AND
    (c) its rows contain dollar amounts in the millions or
        billions (assessed value scale), typically 8–12 rows.

  RIGHT-HALF TYPE R2 — Schedule B right half:
    A page qualifies as a Schedule B continuation if it:
    (a) has no printed schedule title or entity name, AND
    (b) begins with a "Fiscal Year" header followed
        immediately by a row of year numbers such as
        "2020  2021  2022  2023  2024  2025", AND
    (c) its rows contain millage-scale decimal numbers
        (e.g. 5.276, 0.916, 7.107) — typically 3–4 digit
        values with 3 decimal places.

  A page matching TYPE R1 or TYPE R2 is included in TAX_BASE
  regardless of whether it has a schedule title or a
  "(Continued)" label. The absence of a title is EXPECTED
  for these pages and is NOT a reason to exclude them.

TARGET 4 — PEN: Defined Benefit PENSION note and RSI pages
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Return every page that carries pension disclosure for a DEFINED BENEFIT plan, including:
  • the note titled "Pension Plan(s)", "Retirement Plan(s)", "Employee Retirement
    Systems", "Defined Benefit Pension Plans", or similar — ALL its pages;
  • actuarial assumptions for a pension plan (discount rate, long-term expected rate of
    return, inflation, salary increases, mortality table, amortization method);
  • the "Sensitivity of the Net Pension Liability to changes in the discount rate"
    schedule (a 1% decrease / current rate / 1% increase table);
  • the "Changes in the Net Pension Liability" or "Changes in Total Pension Liability"
    roll-forward (rows: Service cost, Interest, Benefit payments, Contributions,
    Net investment income, ...);
  • the Deferred Outflows / Deferred Inflows of Resources schedule RELATED TO PENSIONS,
    including any "contributions subsequent to the measurement date" line;
  • RSI pension schedules: "Schedule of Changes in Net Pension Liability and Related
    Ratios", "Schedule of the Employer's Proportionate Share of the Net Pension
    Liability", "Schedule of Contributions" (pension), and their notes;
  • the "Long-Term Liabilities" / "Changes in Long-Term Obligations" note page that splits
    the net pension liability between Governmental and Business-type activities — this page
    is REQUIRED for the activity-proportion calculation, so include it even though it is
    primarily a DEBT page.

DO NOT include a page whose ONLY pension content is a Defined Contribution or Deferred
Compensation plan (401(a), 403(b), 457) with no defined benefit disclosure.
