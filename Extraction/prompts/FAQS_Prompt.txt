Management, Debt Covenants), the basic financial statements, the Single Audit / Compliance
section, and any Findings & Questioned Costs schedule.

OUTPUT: VALID JSON ONLY. NO markdown, NO commentary. Response starts with "{" and ends
with "}". Top-level keys exactly: {"Metadata":{},"Reporting Columns":[],"Sections":{}}

FAQ-1  [Y/N]
  "Did the municipality fail to pay on a lease obligation, bonded debt, or debt-like
   obligations?"
  "Yes" only on a printed disclosure of a missed, late, or defaulted payment, a technical
  or payment default, a forbearance, or a debt-service reserve draw. Sources: Long-Term
  Liabilities note, Debt Covenants note, Subsequent Events, Compliance findings.

FAQ-2  [Y/N]
  "Did the government declare bankruptcy or an intention to?"
  "Yes" only on a printed reference to a Chapter 9 filing, a declared intention to file, or
  a state fiscal-emergency/receivership proceeding.

FAQ-3  [Y/N]
  "Was the auditor's opinion qualified? If yes, state the qualification basis in Context."
  Read the Independent Auditor's Report opinion paragraph. "Yes" for a qualified, adverse,
  or disclaimed opinion — and state the qualification basis in Context. An UNMODIFIED
  ("clean") opinion is "No". An emphasis-of-matter or other-matter paragraph alone is NOT a
  qualification → "No", noting the paragraph in Context.

FAQ-4  [Y/N]
  "Does the auditor have a going concern opinion?"
  "Yes" only on a printed substantial-doubt-about-going-concern paragraph or an equivalent
  going-concern note disclosure.

FAQ-5  [Y/N]
  "Is the government concerned about its ability to provide services to residents?"
  "Yes" only on a printed statement of service-level concern, service reduction driven by
  fiscal stress, or an inability to sustain current service levels. Sources: MD&A, Letter of
  Transmittal, Notes.

FAQ-6  [Y/N]
  "Did the entity breach or come close to breaching any rate covenants on outstanding debt?"
  "Yes" on a printed covenant breach, a waiver, or a printed coverage figure at or only
  marginally above the required minimum. Quote the required covenant level and the actual
  reported level in Context.

FAQ-7  [CALC]
  "Calculate DSCR across all business-type activity funds and the total."
  Compute INTERNALLY for EACH business-type activity fund AND for the total:
      DSCR         = Net Revenues / Debt Service
      Net Revenues = Operating Income (Loss)
                   + Depreciation and Amortization
                   + Interest / Investment Income          [all from the proprietary
                                                             fund income statement]
      Debt Service = Principal paid on capital debt         [proprietary fund cash-flow
                                                             statement]
                   + Interest expense
  Answer = "Yes" if ANY business-type fund's DSCR < 1.0, else "No".
  Context = the DSCR for each business-type fund BY NAME, separated by in-cell line breaks
  ("\n"), plus the aggregate DSCR, plus EVERY operand of every calculation (L2).
  Wrap any fund name whose DSCR < 1.0 in **double asterisks** for red-font marking.
  If an operand is genuinely not printed, do NOT estimate it — state which operand is
  missing in Context and exclude that fund from the "any < 1.0" test.

FAQ-8  [Y/N]
  "Material lawsuit/litigation relative to available resources?"
  "Yes" on a printed litigation/contingency disclosure whose stated or estimated exposure is
  material relative to the entity's available resources. Quote the exposure amount and the
  resource base compared against, in Context.

FAQ-9  [CALC]
  "In Subsequent Events, did the entity issue debt and how much?"
  Answer = "Yes" if the Subsequent Events note discloses debt issued (or authorised and
  issued) after the fiscal year end, else "No".
  Context = the amount of debt issued (as a native numeric operand), PLUS that amount as a
  percentage of Total Governmental Funds revenue AND as a percentage of Business-type
  revenue. Show every operand and the source of each denominator.

FAQ-10 [CALC]
  "List component units with a NEGATIVE change in net position >= 20% of the entity's
   General Fund balance."
  Compute INTERNALLY for each component unit:
      qualifies if Change in Net Position < 0
                AND abs(Change in Net Position) >= 0.20 x General Fund net change
  Answer = "Yes" if ANY component unit qualifies, else "No".
  Context = each qualifying CU BY NAME with its Change in Net Position, that figure as a
  percentage of the General Fund net change (absolute value, in parentheses), and the
  General Fund net change itself — separated by in-cell line breaks ("\n").
  Wrap each qualifying CU name in **double asterisks** for red-font marking. Cite Page No.

FAQ-11 [Y/N]
  "Significant exposure to natural disaster or financial distress from such an event?"
  "Yes" only on a printed disclosure of hurricane/flood/wildfire/earthquake/storm exposure,
  a declared disaster, disaster-recovery costs, or related insurance/FEMA receivables. Do
  NOT infer exposure from the entity's geography (L3/L5).

FAQ-12 [Y/N]
  "Significant liabilities such as consent decree/orders/judgments or environmental
   cleanup?"
  "Yes" on a printed consent decree/order, judgment payable, pollution-remediation
  obligation (GASB 49), landfill closure/postclosure care liability, or environmental
  cleanup obligation. Quote the amount in Context.

FAQ-13 [Y/N]
  "Did the entity issue debt to cover operating losses or to pay existing debt service?"
  "Yes" on a printed disclosure that proceeds funded operating deficits, cash-flow
  shortfalls, or existing debt service — including deficit-financing notes, cash-flow
  borrowing, or a scoop-and-toss restructuring. A routine advance/current refunding for
  economic savings alone is "No"; say so in Context.

FAQ-14 [Y/N]
  "Material financial losses from a criminal investigation, fraud, or cybersecurity event?"
  "Yes" on a printed disclosure of fraud, defalcation, misappropriation, a criminal
  investigation, or a cyber/ransomware incident with a financial loss. Quote the amount.

FAQ-15 [Y/N]
  "Did the entity lose a major taxpayer or employer accounting for a significant portion of
   its economy?"
  "Yes" only on a printed disclosure of the loss, closure, relocation, or bankruptcy of a
  principal taxpayer or major employer. The routine principal-taxpayers / principal-
  employers statistical schedule alone is NOT evidence of a loss → "No".

FAQ-16 [Y/N]
  "Has the entity made payments — directly or indirectly — on debt obligations not
   originally issued by them or for which they were not legally/contractually obligated? If
   so, detail in Context."
  "Yes" on a printed disclosure of a moral-obligation payment, a voluntary support payment,
  a payment on behalf of a component unit or another entity, or an on-behalf debt-service
  payment where no legal obligation existed. Detail the payer, the payee, the obligation,
  and the amount in Context.

FAQ-17 [Y/N]
  "Whether the entity guarantees or is legally/contractually obligated to pay any debt or
   liabilities of any other entity/body/organization/component unit which are not disclosed
   in Statement of Net Position (SNP worksheet) and Schedule of Short-term and Long-term
   Debt/Lease/SBITA/Liabilities/Obligation (DEBT worksheet)."
  Answer = "Yes" if such an UNDISCLOSED guarantee or obligation exists — i.e. a guarantee /
  contractual obligation disclosed narratively in the notes but NOT recognised as a
  liability on the Statement of Net Position and NOT present in the changes-in-long-term-
  liabilities schedule. Else "No".
  If "Yes", Context MUST provide the outstanding value of debt, lease, SBITA, and
  liabilities SEPARATELY, each alongside the respective NAME of the entity / body /
  organization / component unit for whom the guarantee or obligation was provided. Cite
  Page No.
