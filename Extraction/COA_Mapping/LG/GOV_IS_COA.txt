H. COA DATAPOINT MAPPING (AUTHORITATIVE)
===============================================================================
Every row must have "COA Datapoint". Map using GOV_IS Items (post hierarchy
flattening), COA Flag, current Section, Standard COA Master.

COA MASTER FILTERING (STRICT):
FLAG FILTER: Row maps ONLY to COA Master entries with identical COA Flag.
STATEMENT FILTER: Only use COA Master rows where Statement contains "GOV_IS".
SECTION FILTER: Use the MAPPED master section (see mapping below).

GOV_IS PDF-TO-MASTER SECTION MAPPING:
  PDF "Revenues"                       = Master "Operating Revenues"
  PDF "Expenditures"                   = Master "Operating Expenses"
  PDF "Other Financing Sources (Uses)" = Master "Other Financing Sources"
  PDF "Net Change in Fund Balances"    = Master "Net Change In Fund Balances"

MATCHING ORDER (within same flag):
1) Mandatory mappings below
2) Full exact match (case-insensitive)
3) Whitespace-normalized
4) Punctuation-insensitive
5) Prefix-aware (if ":" in label, match root prefix before first ":")
6) Semantic closest (high confidence only)
7) No match -> apply fallback (H5)

===============================================================================
H1. MANDATORY COA MAPPINGS -- DPG
===============================================================================
PDF DPG "Revenues"                       -> "Operating Revenues"
PDF DPG "Expenditures"                   -> "Operating Expenses"
PDF DPG "Other Financing Sources (Uses)" -> "Other Financing Sources"

===============================================================================
H2. MANDATORY COA MAPPINGS -- CP
===============================================================================
Total revenues | Total Revenues
  -> "Total Operating Revenues"
Total expenditures | Total Expenditures
  -> "Total Operating Expenses"
Excess (deficiency) of revenues over expenditures | Revenues over (under)
expenditures | Excess of revenues over (under) expenditures
  -> "Excess of Revenues Over/Under Expenditures"
Total other financing sources (uses) | Total Other Financing Sources (Uses)
  -> "Total Other Financing Sources"
Net change in fund balances | Net Change in Fund Balances
  -> "Net Change In Fund Balances"
Excess of revenues over (under) expenditures | Excess of Revenues Over/Under Expenditures|Excess Of Revenue|Excess Of Expenditure
  -> "Excess of Revenues Over/Under Expenditures"
===============================================================================
H3. MANDATORY COA MAPPINGS -- DP (REVENUES SECTION)
===============================================================================
The PDF "Revenues" section maps to master section "Operating Revenues".
Match DP labels against master DPs in "Operating Revenues":

General property taxes | Ad valorem taxes | Property taxes 
  (ONLY when label explicitly contains "property" or "ad valorem")
  -> "Property Taxes"

Taxes (bare, unqualified, no "property"/"ad valorem" modifier)
  -> "Other Tax Revenues"
Tax increment collections | Tax increment | TIF revenues
  -> "Property Taxes"
Sales taxes | Sales tax revenues
  -> "Sales Taxes"
Income taxes
  -> "Income Taxes"
Business taxes | Business license taxes
  -> "Business Taxes"
Other city taxes | Other taxes | Franchise taxes | Hotel taxes | Utility taxes
  -> "Other Tax Revenues"
Licenses and permits | Licenses, permits and fees
  -> "Charges for Services"
Charges for services | Charges for service
  -> "Charges for Services"
Fines and forfeitures | Fines and penalties
  -> "Charges for Services"
Intergovernmental | Intergovernmental revenues
  -> "Intergovernmental Revenues"
Use of money and property | Interest and investment earnings | Investment income
  -> "Interest and Investment Earnings"
Miscellaneous | Other revenues | Other
  -> "Other Operating Revenues"

Any other unmatched DP in Revenues section -> "Other_Operating_Revenues"

===============================================================================
H4. MANDATORY COA MAPPINGS -- DP (EXPENDITURES SECTION)
===============================================================================
The PDF "Expenditures" section maps to master section "Operating Expenses".

"Current:" PREFIX RULE: Strip "Current: " before matching. It is a GAAP artifact.
  "Current: General government" -> match "General government" -> "General Government"
  "Current: Public safety"      -> match "Public safety"      -> "Public Safety"
  "Current: Public works"       -> match "Public works"       -> "Public Works"
  "Current: <anything else>"    -> if no match below          -> "Other_Operating_Expenses"

ROOT PREFIX RULE: If label has ":" prefix, extract root and match:
  Root "Debt service"   -> "Debt Service"   (all children get this)
  Root "Capital outlay" -> "Capital Outlay"  (all children get this)

General government -> "General Government"
Public safety -> "Public Safety"
Public works -> "Public Works"
Education -> "Education"
Social services | Health and social services -> "Social Services"
Debt service: <any child> -> "Debt Service"
Capital outlay: <any child> -> "Capital Outlay"
Depreciation | Depreciation and maintenance -> "Depreciation and Maintenance"
Operations and maintenance -> "Operations and Maintenances"

Any other unmatched DP in Expenditures section -> "Other_Operating_Expenses"

===============================================================================
H4B. MANDATORY COA MAPPINGS -- DP (OTHER FINANCING SOURCES SECTION)
===============================================================================
The PDF "Other Financing Sources (Uses)" section maps to master
section "Other Financing Sources".

Transfers in -> "Transfer In"
Transfers out -> "Transfer Out"
Proceeds from sale of capital asset | Sale of capital assets -> "Other Financing Sources"
Bonds issued | Issuance of debt | Issuance of bonds -> "Other Financing Sources"
Premium on bonds issued | Bond premium -> "Other Financing Sources"
Refunding bonds | Payment to refund bond escrow -> "Other Financing Sources"
Initiation of SBITAs | Initiation of leases -> "Other Financing Sources"
Insurance recoveries -> "Other Financing Sources"

Any other unmatched DP in OFS section -> "Other_Other_Financing_Sources"

===============================================================================
H5. FALLBACK RULES (WHEN NO MATCH FOUND ABOVE)
===============================================================================

DP ROWS -- no match after all steps above:
  COA Datapoint = "Other_<DPGName>"
  <DPGName> = COA Datapoint of the nearest preceding DPG row.
  Replace spaces with underscores.

  Fallback values by section:
    Revenues section (DPG = "Operating Revenues")     -> "Other_Operating_Revenues"
    Expenditures section (DPG = "Operating Expenses")  -> "Other_Operating_Expenses"
    OFS section (DPG = "Other Financing Sources")      -> "Other_Other_Financing_Sources"

  DP must NEVER have blank "" COA Datapoint.

CP ROWS -- no match:
  COA Datapoint = "" (blank)
  NEVER invent a CP COA Datapoint.

DPG ROWS -- no match:
  COA Datapoint = "Other_DPG"
  DPG must NEVER have blank "" COA Datapoint.

COA Datapoint must never be null; use "" only for unmatched CP rows.
