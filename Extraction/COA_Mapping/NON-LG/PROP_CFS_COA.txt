H. COA DATAPOINT MAPPING (AUTHORITATIVE)
===============================================================================
Every row must have "COA Datapoint". Map using PROP_CFS Items (post hierarchy
flattening), COA Flag, current Section, Standard COA Master.

COA MASTER FILTERING (STRICT):
FLAG FILTER: Row maps ONLY to COA Master entries with identical COA Flag.
STATEMENT FILTER: Only use COA Master rows where Statement contains "CFS".
SECTION FILTER: Use the MAPPED master section (see mapping below).

PROP_CFS PDF-TO-MASTER SECTION MAPPING:
  PDF "Cash Flows from Operating Activities"                     = Master "Cash Flows from Operating Activities"
  PDF "Cash Flows from Noncapital Financing Activities"          = Master "Cash Flows from Noncapital Financing Activities"
  PDF "Cash Flows from Capital and Related Financing Activities" = Master "Cash Flows from Capital and Related Financing Activities"
  PDF "Cash Flows from Investing Activities"                     = Master "Cash Flows from Investing Activities"
  PDF "Net Change in Cash and Cash Equivalents"                  = Master "Net Change in Cash and Cash Equivalents"
  PDF "Balance of Cash and Cash Equivalents"                     = Master "Cash and Cash Equivalents"

MATCHING ORDER (within same flag):
1) Mandatory mappings below
2) Full exact match (case-insensitive)
3) No match -> apply fallback (H5)

H3. MANDATORY COA MAPPINGS -- DP (CRITICAL RULE FOR SECTIONS 1-4)
===============================================================================

*** THIS IS THE KEY RULE FOR PROP_CFS ***

For sections 1-4 (Operating, Noncapital, Capital, Investing):
ALL DP rows get the SAME COA Datapoint as their parent DPG.
There is NO individual DP mapping for cash flow line items.

The COA Master has only ONE DP entry per activity section, and its COA Datapoint
is identical to the DPG name. Therefore:

EVERY DP in "Cash Flows from Operating Activities"
  -> COA Datapoint = "Cash Flows from Operating Activities"

EVERY DP in "Cash Flows from Noncapital Financing Activities"
  -> COA Datapoint = "Cash Flows from Noncapital Financing Activities"

EVERY DP in "Cash Flows from Capital and Related Financing Activities"
  -> COA Datapoint = "Cash Flows from Capital and Related Financing Activities"

EVERY DP in "Cash Flows from Investing Activities"
  -> COA Datapoint = "Cash Flows from Investing Activities"

This applies regardless of the line item label. Whether the DP is
"Receipts from customers", "Payments to employees", "Acquisition of capital assets",
"Interest paid", "Investment income" — they ALL get the same COA Datapoint
as their parent section DPG.

H4. MANDATORY COA MAPPINGS -- DP (BALANCE SECTION ONLY)
===============================================================================

The "Balance of Cash and Cash Equivalents" section has DISTINCT DP mapping:

Beginning of year | Cash and cash equivalents, July 1 | Beginning balance
  -> "Beginning Balance of Cash and Cash Equivalents"

Ending of year | Cash and cash equivalents, June 30 | Ending balance
  -> "Ending Balance of Cash and Cash Equivalents"
  (NOTE: If "End of year" is classified as CP, it maps via H2 instead)

===============================================================================
H5. FALLBACK RULES (WHEN NO MATCH FOUND)
===============================================================================

DP ROWS -- no match:
  COA Datapoint = "Other_<DPGName>"
  <DPGName> = COA Datapoint of the nearest preceding DPG row.
  Replace spaces with underscores.
  DP must NEVER have blank "" COA Datapoint.

  NOTE: For sections 1-4, this fallback should NEVER trigger because H3
  assigns ALL DPs the same COA Datapoint as their DPG. The fallback exists
  only as a safety net.

CP ROWS -- no match:
  COA Datapoint = "" (blank)
  NEVER invent a CP COA Datapoint.

DPG ROWS -- no match:
  COA Datapoint = "Other_DPG"
  DPG must NEVER have blank "" COA Datapoint.

COA Datapoint must never be null; use "" only for unmatched CP rows.
