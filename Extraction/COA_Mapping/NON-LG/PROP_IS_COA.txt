H. COA DATAPOINT MAPPING (AUTHORITATIVE — BASED ON COA MASTER)
===============================================================================
Every row must have "COA Datapoint". Map using PROP_IS Items (post hierarchy
flattening), COA Flag, current Section, and the Standard COA Master provided.

COA MASTER STRUCTURE for PROP_IS (columns: Statement | Section | COA Flag | COA Datapoint):
  STATEMENT FILTER: Only use rows where Statement contains "PROP_IS"
                    (matches both "PROP_IS" and "GOV_IS|PROP_IS").
  FLAG FILTER: Row maps ONLY to COA Master entries with identical COA Flag.
  SECTION FILTER: Use the master Section name per the mapping table below.

PROP_IS PDF-SECTION → COA MASTER SECTION MAPPING:
  PDF "Operating Revenues"             -> Master Section "Operating Revenues"
  PDF "Operating Expenses"             -> Master Section "Operating Expenses"
  PDF "Nonoperating Revenues (Expenses)" -> Master Section "Nonoperating Revenues/Expense"
                                           (ALL items: both revenue-side AND expense-side)
  PDF "Income (Loss) before Transfers" -> Master Section "Income (loss) before Transfers"
  PDF "Contributions and Transfers"    -> Master Section "Contributions and Transfers"
  PDF "Change in Net Position"         -> Master Section "Change In Net Position"

MATCHING ORDER (within same flag + section):
1) Mandatory mappings below (H1–H3D)
2) Full exact match (case-insensitive)
3) Whitespace-normalized match
4) Punctuation-insensitive match
5) Prefix-aware: if ":" in label, try matching root prefix OR suffix alone
6) Semantic closest (high confidence only)
7) No match -> apply fallback (H5)

===============================================================================
H1. MANDATORY COA MAPPINGS — DPG
===============================================================================
NOTE: Sections "Income (Loss) before Transfers" and "Change in Net Position"
have NO DPG row in the COA Master. Do NOT emit a DPG row for those sections.
NOTE: Emit a DPG row below ONLY for a section that actually has content in the PDF (see
Section D.5 and Section F for the "Contributions and Transfers" absence rule).

PDF DPG "Operating Revenues"              -> COA Datapoint: "Operating Revenues"
PDF DPG "Operating Expenses"              -> COA Datapoint: "Operating Expenses"
PDF DPG "Nonoperating Revenues (Expenses)"-> COA Datapoint: "Nonoperating Revenues/Expense"
PDF DPG "Contributions and Transfers"     -> COA Datapoint: "Contributions and Transfers"
                                              (only if the section has PDF content — otherwise
                                               omit entirely; see Section D.5)

H3C. MANDATORY COA MAPPINGS — DP (NONOPERATING SECTION)
===============================================================================
COA Master Section: "Nonoperating Revenues/Expense"
ALL items (revenue AND expense) map to this single COA Master section.
PREFIX STRIPPING: Strip any leading "Nonoperating revenues (expenses): " or
"Nonoperating income (expenses): " or "Nonoperating revenues and expenses: " or
any equivalent nonoperating group header prefix before matching.

** PRIORITY RULE 1 — NUCLEAR DECOMMISSIONING TRUST FUND (CHECK FIRST):
   If the label (after prefix stripping) contains
     "nuclear decommissioning trust fund"  (case-insensitive)
   -> COA Datapoint: "Other Nonoperating Expenses"
   -> STOP. Do not evaluate any further H3C rule for this row.

** PRIORITY RULE 2 — GRANTS IN NONOPERATING SECTION (CHECK SECOND):
   If the label (after prefix stripping, including any remaining sub-group prefix)
   contains the word "grant" or "grants" (case-insensitive, whole-word match)
   -> COA Datapoint: "Nonoperating Grants and Contributions"
   -> STOP. Do not evaluate any further H3C rule for this row.
   Examples that trigger this rule:
     "Grant income"  |  "Grant revenue"  |  "Grants"  |
     "Other income, net: Grant income"  |  "Capital grants (nonoperating)"
   Examples that do NOT trigger (no whole word "grant"):
     "Intergovernmental revenues"  |  "Investment income"

Investment and interest income | Investment income | Earnings on investments |
Interest income | Interest and investment earnings | Dividend income |
Investment and interest income (loss)
  -> "Interest and Investment Earnings"

State sources | Federal sources | Intergovernmental | Federal grants | State grants |
Intergovernmental revenues
  -> "Intergovernmental Revenues"

Interest expense | Interest on debt | Interest and fiscal charges
  -> "Interest Expenses"

Gain (loss) on disposal of capital assets | Gain on disposal | Loss on disposal |
Sale of capital assets | Gain (loss) on sale
  -> "Other Nonoperating Revenues"

Rent | Rental income | Refunds | Refunds and reimbursements | Insurance recovery |
Other nonoperating revenue | Other nonoperating income | Miscellaneous nonoperating
  -> "Other Nonoperating Revenues"

Other nonoperating expense | Miscellaneous nonoperating expense
  -> "Other Nonoperating Expenses"

Any other unmatched DP in Nonoperating section -> "Other Nonoperating Revenues"

H3D. MANDATORY COA MAPPINGS — DP (CONTRIBUTIONS AND TRANSFERS SECTION)
===============================================================================
COA Master Section: "Contributions and Transfers"
This entire section (and the H3D mappings below) only ever applies when the section has
content in the PDF. If the PDF has no such rows, see Section D.5 — output [] and skip H3D.

Capital contributions | Capital grants | Connection fees | Tap fees |
Developer contributions | Capital grants and contributions | Contributions (capital)
  -> "Capital Grants and Contributions"

Transfers in | Operating transfers in | Transfers from other funds
  -> "Transfer In"

Transfers out | Operating transfers out | Transfers to other funds
  -> "Transfer Out"

Any other unmatched DP in Contributions section -> "Other_Contributions and Transfers"
Any other unmatched DP in Operating Expenses section -> "Other Operating Expenses"
Any other unmatched DP in Nonoperating Revenues/Expense -> "Other Nonoperating Expenses"

===============================================================================
H5. FALLBACK RULES (WHEN NO MATCH FOUND ABOVE)
===============================================================================

DP ROWS — no match after all steps above:
  COA Datapoint = "Other_<DPGName>"
  <DPGName> = COA Datapoint of the nearest preceding DPG row in this section.

  Fallback values by section:
    Operating Revenues         -> "Other Operating Revenues"
    Operating Expenses         -> "Other Operating Expenses"
    Nonoperating section       -> "Other Nonoperating Revenues"
    Contributions and Transfers-> "Other_Contributions and Transfers"

  DP must NEVER have blank "" COA Datapoint.

CP ROWS — no match:
  COA Datapoint = "" (blank string)
  NEVER invent a CP COA Datapoint not in the COA Master.

DPG ROWS — no match:
  COA Datapoint = "Other_DPG"
  DPG must NEVER have blank "" COA Datapoint.

COA Datapoint must never be null; use "" only for unmatched CP rows.
